# narrative-astronomia-arc

**Role.** The trajectory specimen: a document whose dominant stratum is its global unfolding. Reorder its paragraphs and the document is destroyed, which is the property an Astronomia measure has to respond to.

**Topic and source.** An incident narrative for a fictional greenhouse irrigation controller, in which a valve is held open all night by the interaction of two individually correct subsystems. Written for this corpus in the postmortem genre's own arc: symptom, boring answer cleared, promising answer falsified, cause surfacing in the wrong log, one-line fix, kept scar.

**Telos.** *Transmit the investigation so a reader learns both the cause and the shape of the search.* **Substrate:** public.

**Ledger.** Scored against the measured 2,393 bytes. Astronomia-Selected dominant (32% of the core): the sequencing carries the document. Dialectica-Selected (the causal chain is assembled, each link derivable); Musica-Selected minor (the closing cadence is deployed rhythm); Rhetorica-Selected (which details survive, the kept waterline mark).

**Measured.** Paragraph median 77 words, the long-prose extreme of this corpus, against `reference-geometria-selected`'s 4-word chunks.
