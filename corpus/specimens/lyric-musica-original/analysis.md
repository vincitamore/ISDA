# lyric-musica-original

**Role.** The Musica-Original calibration case: a text whose irreducible work lives in rhythm, not claims. It exercises all four rungs of the intensity ladder in one document, and it is the text a mechanical Musica measure has to place high: `musica.surprisal-variance` puts it in the top quartile of block B (working markdown).

**Topic and source.** Gerard Manley Hopkins, "Pied Beauty" (1877, pub. 1918, public domain in the US). The body is copied by byte range from `examples.md` Example 1 by `scripts/extract-worked-examples.py`; the ledger is scored against the measured 577 bytes.

**Telos.** Framing A of the worked example: *transmit the observation that dappled, irregular, mixed things point toward an unchanging source*. **Substrate:** public (English literacy, general cultural awareness).

**Ledger.** From `examples.md` Example 1, Step 2 (Framing A). The full worked analysis (line-level rollup, the Framing B contrast, the compression curve) is in `examples.md`; this specimen carries the claims that can run.
