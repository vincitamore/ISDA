# markup-heavy-strip-sensitivity

**Role.** The strip-sensitivity specimen: a document in which most bytes are markup, so every prose statistic depends on the markup exclusion working. Counted as prose, a page like this contributes `| |---|---|` fragments to the word total and to the candidate list.

**Topic and source.** A CLI reference page for a fictional seed-tray scheduler (`potting-bench`), written for this corpus: frontmatter, two flag tables, fenced and inline code, a relative link. The standard furniture of working tool documentation.

**Telos.** *Reference: let a user look up a flag and its default without reading prose.* **Substrate:** public.

**Ledger.** Scored against the measured 1,909 bytes. Geometria-Selected dominant: the page's value is its layout (tables to scan, defaults in a column). Rhetorica-Selected minor (which flags get a prose warning). All content strata Stock.

**Measured.** 136 prose words of 316 whitespace tokens: 57% of the source is markup. Chunk median 11.5 (layout), paragraph median 16.5 (prose). The gap between the two is what the two shape measures separate on a markup-heavy page.
