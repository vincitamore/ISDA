# correspondence-short-chunk

**Role.** The correspondence specimen: the email-thread shape (sender lines, short paragraphs, message separators). It holds the conversational short-chunk region of the layout spectrum and the substrate-tier boundary: a thread reads differently against its participants' shared context than against the public web.

**Topic and source.** A three-message scheduling thread between two fictional greenhouse volunteers, written for this corpus.

**Telos.** *Settle a date and sequence the Saturday work.* **Substrate:** context, the two participants' shared operation, which the thread presupposes throughout.

**Ledger.** Scored against the measured 1,108 bytes. Rhetorica-Selected dominant (what each writer chooses to ask, answer and add); Dialectica-Implied (the sequencing logic, relay before trays and wiring with pressure down, is derivable from what both parties know). Everything else Stock.

**Measured.** Chunk median 9.0 words: conversational short chunks, the same region as block B's emails (about 11.5 words per chunk).
