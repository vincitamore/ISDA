# argumentative-dialectica-original

**Role.** The Dialectica-Original case: argumentative prose whose irreducible work is originated claims, not rhythm or architecture. One stratum carries about 87% of the core, which makes ND nearly a single-stratum readout.

**Topic and source.** "The Problem of Defaults", a 1,058-word exploratory essay on default choices in system design, written for this framework's examples. The body is copied by byte range from `examples.md` Example 2 by `scripts/extract-worked-examples.py`.

**Telos.** Framing A of the worked example: *deliver the central claim about defaults to a policy-interested general reader*. **Substrate:** public.

**Ledger.** From `examples.md` Example 2, Step 2 (Framing A). Rhetorica's row reads "~60 B + ~4 bits" in the prose; the ledger carries 60, and the bits stay in the analysis. The total is 1,610 B. The paragraph rollup and compression curve are in `examples.md`.
