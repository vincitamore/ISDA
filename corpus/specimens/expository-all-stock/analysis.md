# expository-all-stock

**Role.** The correct-zero case: expository prose whose telos is efficient transfer of established knowledge, where any originality would be a defect. It exercises the framework's two exact boundary values, RI at its 1.0 ceiling and ND at its 0 floor, and the genre-conditional reading of SCR (29.7 is right for pedagogy and would be bloat for argument).

**Topic and source.** "How Photosynthesis Works", an 861-word expository passage written for this framework's examples. The body is copied by byte range from `examples.md` Example 3 by `scripts/extract-worked-examples.py`.

**Telos.** *Teach the essential mechanism of photosynthesis to a biology student who knows basic chemistry, such that they could answer an exam question and understand where this fits in biology.* **Substrate:** public (high-school chemistry literacy).

**Ledger.** From `examples.md` Example 3, Step 2. Rhetorica's "~35 B + ~15 bits" carries 35 in the arithmetic. The total is 190 B against a raw length of 5,643.

**Boundary values.** Seven Stock rows force RI to exactly 1.0 and ND to exactly 0, so the two metric assertions carry zero tolerance.
